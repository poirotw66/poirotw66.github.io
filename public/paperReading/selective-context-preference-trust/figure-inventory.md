## Selection rationale

The pair embeds five distinct material figures per language. Figures 1 and 2 establish the phenomenon and benchmark construction; Figure 3 teaches the method's matched quartet; Figure 7 connects the headline result to repair and ablation diagnostics; Figure 8 covers subgroup/source slices that qualify the headline. Figure 4's coverage distribution and Figure 5's compact transfer plot are discussed in prose, while Figure 6's human audit is summarized because the five selected placements already cover the central evidence path without repeating the same result.

The cover is deliberately excluded from this inventory's body-figure count. It is an Evidence Atlas visual generated from the paper's evidence relationship, not a paper figure and not an additional experimental claim.
