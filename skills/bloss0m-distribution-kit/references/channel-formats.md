# Channel formats

## LinkedIn

- 120–220 words.
- Open with the engineering tension or decision, not “new post”.
- Use short paragraphs, one compact list at most, and one article CTA.
- Prefer 0–3 specific hashtags.

## Email digest

- Subject: 35–60 characters.
- Preview text: one concrete reader benefit.
- Body: 80–160 words with problem, judgment, limitation, and CTA.

## Short hooks

- 20–45 words each.
- Produce one decision hook, one counterintuitive finding, and one practical consequence.
- Keep each useful without clickbait.

## Tracking

Use:

`utm_source=<channel>&utm_medium=<social|email|referral>&utm_campaign=<article-slug>&utm_content=<variant>`

Keep lowercase ASCII values. Use stable channel names so analytics can compare campaigns.
