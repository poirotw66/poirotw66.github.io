## Editorial fit

- Reader question: After Lewis RAG made generation condition on retrieved passages, is the missing control point whether / when to retrieve and how to critique generations—without treating reflection tokens as a production RAG gate?
- Why this belongs in the selected track: fills the named when-to-retrieve hole on the RAG method-foundation map (blog 92), on the retrieval-systems path after Lewis RAG (31) and before 2025 leaves.
- Gap it fills: adaptive retrieval + self-critique via reflection tokens, contrasted with always-retrieve Lewis RAG and procedural Read-Gate (note 15).
- Why now: blog 92 already marked Self-RAG as the remaining named hole; notes 31–32 are on main.

## Claim map

- Problem: Always-retrieve RAG can inject irrelevant passages and hurt versatility; models are not trained to follow or critique retrieved evidence.
- Main claim: Train one LM with reflection tokens (Retrieve / ISREL / ISSUP / ISUSE) for on-demand retrieval and self-critique; 7B/13B beat several retrieval-augmented baselines on PopQA, PubHealth, ARC, bio FactScore, and ASQA citation metrics (Table 2).
- Method: GPT-4 silver critic → distill critic → augment generator data with passages + tokens (~145k) → inference with adaptive threshold and critique-guided beam; Contriever-MS MARCO over Wikipedia.
- What is genuinely new: retrieval becomes a learned decision inside the generator vocabulary, not a fixed pipeline stage.

## Evidence audit

- Datasets: PopQA (long-tail 1,399), TriviaQA-unfiltered, PubHealth, ARC-Challenge, biography (FactScore), ALCE-ASQA.
- Benchmarks and metrics: accuracy / containment; FactScore; ASQA str-em, rouge, MAUVE, citation precision/recall.
- Baselines: Llama2, Alpaca, Llama2-FT, ChatGPT, Ret-ChatGPT, Ret-Llama2-chat, SAIL*, Toolformer*, CoVE*, perplexity.ai.
- Ablations: Table 3a No Retriever / No Critic / No retrieval / Hard constraints / Retrieve top1 / Remove ISSUP (50k); Figure 3b ISSUP weight; Figure 3c threshold δ; Figure 4 data scale + 50-example human study.
- Statistical uncertainty: main tables are point estimates; human study n=50 per task.
- Threats to validity: silver GPT-4 labels; 50k ablations vs full 150k main models; ASQA ablation on 150 examples; Wikipedia memory; tokens can be wrong.

## Critical reading

- Strongest result: Table 2 multi-task gains plus Table 3a showing No Critic and Retrieve top1 hurt; Figure 3c shows adaptive retrieval frequency is controllable.
- Weakest assumption: that GPT-4 silver reflection labels are stable enough to become a control surface.
- Stated limitations: generations may still lack full citation support (Ethical Concerns).
- Claims not supported by the evidence: production ACL/citation platforms; agent tool loops; Read-Gate procedural metrics; later leaf or 2025–26 agentic RAG numbers.
