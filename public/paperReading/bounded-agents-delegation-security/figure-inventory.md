## Selection notes

- The paper exposes four original figures in the v1 HTML. Figures 1–3 are selected because they are taught directly in both language versions and cover architecture, mechanism, and empirical evidence.
- Figure 4 is not embedded because its deterministic benchmark result is explained in prose and tables; omitting it does not remove a material claim from the reading. The body-figure gate is satisfied by three distinct original figures per language.
- The three local SVG files are shared by the Traditional Chinese and English articles to keep bilingual figure paths and counts identical.
- `title_image.webp` is a separate, original Evidence Atlas cover. It is not a paper figure and is not counted here.
