## Decide readiness before creating article files

- **Selected, needs research:** an exploration lead, incomplete evidence, unresolved identity, or stale source state. Read the full relevant primary material and artifacts, audit claims and gaps, then honestly re-evaluate. Do not add points to meet a gate or call a lead qualified.
- **Eligible for drafting:** the user has selected the named topic/form and the evidence is sufficient. For Frontier Watch material retain the formal gates: Blog 23–25/25, evidence >=3 and readerInterest >=4; Paper v2 28–30/30, evidence >=3, with unscored series fit. A direct source commission needs the same source/claim audit and must not masquerade as an approved Radar record. If it is not eligible, report the specific missing evidence and a research next step, not a fabricated complete article. A threshold policy change is a separate editorial decision.
- **Local draft:** create paired files only within the authorized task, preserve routes/other edits, and label unresolved items in the handoff. Evidence audits, semantic teach-back and reader-facing editing are needed before publication-ready status. Covers and final assets are readiness requirements, not a reason to invent evidence or interrupt source research.
- **Publication-ready is not publication-authorized:** a passing validator, a formal candidate, `want-write`, an existing local draft or topic approval does not authorize ledger import, commit, push, PR, merge, deployment or publication. Perform only separately requested actions. Never mark published on local validation alone.

In an inbox-based commission, ledger/brief updates require an explicit named import/update decision and safe worktree state; do not automatically create or modify a shared Radar record as a writing side effect. Preserve existing human notes and approval state.

## Match form and classification honestly

A Blog answers one reader question using the necessary mechanism and evidence; it may be a creative observation, tool exploration, engineering explanation or case analysis. A Paper Reading teaches the source paper's argument with sufficient conceptual detail to reconstruct it; a paper being the source does not automatically require the Paper format.

Choose the evidence profile from the actual paper: `empirical`, `systems`, `theoretical`, `conceptual`, or `mixed`. Store optional `paperType` consistently in both frontmatters; absent keeps the empirical archive default. A label never replaces evidence: theoretical work still needs assumptions and a reasoned argument, conceptual work needs defined entities/relations and grounded examples, and empirical claims still need measurement settings. Mixed work retains the empirical checks and semantic review of any formal claims.

Check website taxonomy separately from Radar tracks. A Radar `cross-domain` track is not a valid website topic ID by itself. If no existing `topics` or category truthfully fits, return `needs-classification` with a narrowly described proposal and affected validator/schema files; stop before creating article files or assigning a misleading Agent-evaluation topic. Do not silently add a topic/tag, change schema, or pad another article to satisfy a taxonomy count. User approval of a classification change is a separate scoped action.
