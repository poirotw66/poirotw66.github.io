# Bloss0m Distribution Kit

Convert one finished article into channel-native copy while preserving its evidence and editorial judgment.

## Required input

- Read both published-language files and use their canonical routes.
- Read [references/channel-formats.md](references/channel-formats.md).
- If either article is unfinished or materially inconsistent, stop and route the work back to `publish-bilingual-ai-blog`.

## Workflow

1. Extract the article's central engineering decision, two supporting facts, one limitation, intended reader, and next action.
2. Create a Traditional Chinese package first, then localize it into independent English copy.
3. Produce only the channels the user requests. If none are named, provide:
   - one LinkedIn post per language;
   - one email digest block per language;
   - three short hooks per language;
   - a UTM plan using the published canonical URL.
4. Keep every quantitative or availability claim within the source article's verified scope.
5. Link to the article once per channel and use one primary CTA. Prefer reading the guide, exploring the case study, or joining the email pilot over generic engagement bait.
6. Label suggested tracking parameters separately from the public copy.

## Guardrails

- Do not manufacture urgency, personal experience, benchmarks, endorsements, or quotes.
- Do not translate sentence by sentence.
- Do not reuse the same opening across all channels.
- Avoid more than three hashtags and omit them when they do not improve discovery.
- Do not publish, schedule, email, or post without explicit authorization.
