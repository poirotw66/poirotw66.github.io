## Claim map

- Primary claim: Frontier-lab progress can be made more legible by publishing AI-led R&D, agent oversight, and compute-allocation measurements with methodology.
- Measured evidence: Anthropic reports Claude leading 26% of measured AI R&D work, more than 90% at or above “collaborates,” roughly 30,000 agents on its measured internal platform, 100% online/offline monitor coverage for that platform, about 0.002% online blocking, and approximately 6% of AI R&D compute allocated to safety in the sampled week.
- Vendor or author claims requiring qualification: The measurements use Anthropic's internal data, models, task taxonomy, and classifiers; they are a snapshot, not independent cross-lab evidence.
- Bloss0m engineering consequence: Treat coverage, review latency, escalation rate, and automation level as operational metrics with definitions and audit trails, not as capability marketing numbers.

## Evidence audit

- Primary evidence inspected: Anthropic's methodology page, measurement definitions, internal snapshot, appendix, and limitations.
- Baseline or comparison: The page proposes time-series and cross-lab comparison rather than a controlled external benchmark; the useful baseline is the reproducible measurement protocol itself.
- Missing evidence: Independent data access, common cross-lab taxonomy, external replication, long-term trends, and validation of internal labels.
