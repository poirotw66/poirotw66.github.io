As one of the most popular sports globally, football's digital expansion is an inevitable trend. A senior executive at the Bundesliga's digital department (DFL Digital) shared that working at the Bundesliga felt like fulfilling a childhood football dream. Since joining the then-startup DFL Digital in 2012, he firmly believed that expanding the digital landscape was the future of the sport.

Today, the Bundesliga is turning this digital vision into reality through a strategic alliance with AWS (Amazon Web Services), leveraging Generative AI to create an unprecedented viewing experience for fans worldwide.

## 1. Ten Years in the Making: Fanbase Exceeds 1 Billion

Through years of dedication, the Bundesliga's global fanbase has surpassed a staggering 1 billion. AWS's technical support has been instrumental behind this impressive achievement.

Together, they have pushed the Bundesliga to the forefront of technological application, constantly exploring and adopting innovative technologies. The core objective is to continually enhance the fan experience, making viewers around the world feel the thrill as if they were right in the stadium.

## 2. Bundesliga Match Facts: Deep Data Analysis Captivates the World

In the collaboration between the Bundesliga and AWS, "Bundesliga Match Facts" is undoubtedly one of the most well-known and popular innovations.

By mining the latest data insights, the Bundesliga provides fans with exclusive and in-depth statistical analysis. These objective data points are no longer just cold numbers; they are transformed into exciting viewing elements. Data shows that **over 90% of international fans believe Bundesliga Match Facts significantly enhances their viewing enjoyment**, proving the immense value of data analysis in modern sports events.

## 3. AI Live Ticker: Multi-Language Real-Time Broadcasting Breaks Geographical Barriers

To further close the distance between football and every fan globally, the Bundesliga has actively introduced Generative AI technology, launching the "AI Live Ticker" feature.

### Authentic Match Presentation
The core concept of the AI Live Ticker is to deliver match updates in the most natural and relatable way possible. It can broadcast match progress in real-time in multiple languages. This feature goes far beyond simple machine translation; it is dedicated to authentically presenting the tension and excitement of the game to fans all over the world.

### A Truly Global Sporting Event
By breaking down language and geographical barriers, the AI Live Ticker makes the Bundesliga a truly global sporting event. No matter where fans are or what language they speak, they can engage with the match with zero distance.

## 4. Embracing Innovation: Co-Creating the New Era of Football

The Bundesliga has consistently maintained an attitude of actively seeking breakthroughs and taking risks to continuously innovate for the new generation of fans. This bold corporate culture perfectly aligns with AWS's philosophy. Sharing a common vision, both parties are committed to creating a more engaging and participatory future for football fans worldwide.

This collaboration between the traditional sports industry and a top-tier cloud technology giant not only redefines the fan viewing model but also sets a new standard for digital transformation in the global sports industry, signaling a broader horizon for AI applications in sports.
